ABOUT US
Our Story
Founded in 2018, The Rightway Center of Learning and Academic Development Ltd. was established to redefine what modern education could look like in Trinidad and Tobago. Built on the philosophy that “The only way is the Rightway,” the institution was created to provide families with a learning environment that is academically rigorous, technologically advanced, values-driven, and intentionally designed around the holistic development of the child.
Today, Rightway has evolved into one of the most sought-after private educational institutions in Central Trinidad with intent to offer its services globally, recognized for being elite without being elitist and for delivering exceptional academic outcomes within a nurturing, disciplined, and future-focused environment.
The Rightway curriculum draws inspiration from Finnish, Chinese, European, American, and Canadian educational systems, blending global best practices into a unique learning model that prioritizes critical thinking, leadership, creativity, entrepreneurship, and real-world application.
At Rightway, students learn through hands-on, tactile, and experiential learning approaches supported by meaningful technology integration, STEM exposure, project-based learning, and interactive instruction. The institution has intentionally reduced dependency on excessive homework and overloaded textbooks, focusing instead on deep understanding, classroom engagement, and balanced child development.
A defining feature of the Rightway philosophy is early financial awareness, entrepreneurship, and investment education through its proprietary Seven Figure Kids Club, where students are exposed to budgeting, leadership, investing, and wealth-building principles from an early age.
Rightway also recognizes the pressures associated with high-stakes examinations and therefore progressively develops students academically from Preschool through Standard Five, allowing children to excel without sacrificing emotional wellbeing, confidence, or creativity.
With small class environments, strong supervision systems, exceptional student support, and a culture grounded in discipline, respect, innovation, and holistic development, Rightway continues to prepare students not only for examinations, but for leadership and life.
