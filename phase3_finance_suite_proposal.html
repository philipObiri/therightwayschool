01 Why This Module

Stop managing school finances in spreadsheets.

Most schools in Trinidad & Tobago track fees, payroll, and expenses across a patchwork of Excel files, WhatsApp messages, and handwritten ledgers. There's no audit trail, no real-time picture of cash flow, and no easy way to produce financial statements for the board or Ministry.

This module brings a complete double-entry accounting system into your school platform — the same accounting logic that powers corporate finance software, purpose-built for a school environment. Every fee collected, every salary processed, every expense recorded — in one place, with export-ready reports on demand.

📒

Full Accounting

Chart of Accounts, General Ledger, Journal Entries, Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet.

💼

Payroll

Staff salary processing, deductions (tax, NIS, health surcharge), payslip generation and storage per pay period.

🏦

Bank Reconciliation

Match bank statements to recorded transactions. Spot discrepancies instantly. Full reconciliation audit trail.
