The Rightway Center Admissions Policy Addendum
Effective Date: 02 Sep 2025
Approved By: Academic Board & School Leadership Team
Purpose
This addendum outlines the extended criteria under which a child may be declined admission to The Rightway Center. It supports our commitment to academic excellence, safety, digital readiness, and values alignment.
1. Academic Readiness and Learning Profile
Below-Level Academic Performance: A student may be declined if diagnostic or entrance assessments reveal significant gaps in literacy or numeracy that cannot be remediated within a mainstream classroom. The child is one or more years below the expected level for their age with no documented support plan.
Learning Support Needs Beyond School Capacity: Applicants may be denied if the child has complex learning disabilities (e.g., severe ASD, global developmental delay) requiring interventions the school is not equipped to provide. There is no diagnostic report, or the parent refuses to collaborate with external specialists. Behavioural or sensory needs disrupt classroom safety or function despite accommodations during the trial.
2. Digital Literacy and Technology Readiness
Rightway is a digitally focused school. Admission may be denied if a student shows extreme difficulty adapting to digital tools (e.g., refuses to use tablets, cannot follow onscreen instructions).
Parents express resistance to technology integration, smart boards, or online communication platforms.
The home environment lacks the infrastructure to support blended learning (e.g., no internet access, consistent device unavailability) without a plan for resolution.
3. Behavioural and Emotional Considerations
Severe Behavioural Disruption: Applications may be declined if a child exhibits persistent aggression, elopement (running off), or deliberate destruction of property during trial or observation. Previous schools have documented multiple suspensions or expulsions for behaviour.
Parent Non-Disclosure of Behavioural History: Applications may be voided if parents fail to disclose previous diagnoses, suspensions, or reports that affect school safety or classroom dynamics. The child is known to require one-on-one supervision, and the parent refuses to acknowledge or support such needs.
4. Documentation and Compliance Issues
Incomplete or Fraudulent Documentation: Admissions may be declined if the application package is missing key documents such as birth certificate, immunization records, academic reports, or parent ID. Documents provided are found to be altered, forged, or inconsistent with known records.
Non-Compliance with School Regulations: Applications may be declined if families repeatedly disregard admissions deadlines, financial requirements, or trial protocols. Parents exhibit disruptive, threatening, or abusive conduct toward staff or the admissions process.
5. Parental Partnership and Support
Rightway values strong home–school collaboration. Admission may be denied if parents show minimal interest or engagement during the orientation or interview process.
The family has a track record of frequent school changes, complaints, or litigation with other institutions.
A parent expresses views contrary to the school's mission, such as refusal to support inclusive education, discipline policies, or character development goals.
6. Financial Capacity and Commitment
Fee Default History: Applications may be declined if there is previous fee delinquency with The Rightway Center or other educational institutions without clear resolution.
Parents are unable or unwilling to meet tuition obligations and reject offered payment plans or scholarships.
Misalignment with Program Expectations: Admission may also be paused if the family applies to a program level that requires financial commitment beyond tuition and they are unable to support participation.
7. Space Availability and Strategic Enrollment
Level or Program Oversubscription: If a class or specialty programme has reached capacity, applicants may be deferred or declined until space becomes available.
Prioritization Criteria: In cases of oversubscription, priority will be given to siblings of current students, alumni families, children with aligned learning profiles, and families who attend orientation and complete all requirements in full.
8. Safeguarding and Legal Concerns
Admission will be denied if a child has been involved in incidents that pose a risk to other students’ safety.
There is an active custody dispute, travel ban, or legal issue that creates uncertainty over school attendance or guardianship.
There are safeguarding concerns raised by previous institutions or community partners that cannot be resolved before enrollment.
Final Notes
This addendum ensures transparency and consistency in The Rightway Center's admissions process. All decisions are made in the best interest of the child, the school community, and the integrity of the institution.
Rightway reserves the right to update or amend this policy as required.
