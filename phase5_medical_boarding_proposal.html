01 Why This Module

Specialist operations need specialist systems.

If your school has a sick bay, boarding house, or guesthouse, you carry responsibilities that go beyond the typical school. Patient records must be accurate and accessible in an emergency. Boarding learners need structured check-in/out and leave management. Guesthouse rooms require booking, payment tracking, and guest communication.

Generic software isn't designed for these functions. This module provides purpose-built management tools for each of these specialist areas — integrated directly into your school platform so information flows where it's needed, when it's needed.

🏥

Medical / Sick Bay

Patient admissions, medical records, medication inventory, low-stock alerts, and emergency health info per learner — available to nursing staff at any time.

🏠

Boarding Management

Dorm assignments, learner check-in/out, boarding leave requests with approvals, incident reports, and dorm supervisor portal.

🏨

Guesthouse & Reception

Room bookings, payment tracking, expense management, inventory, parent communication logs, and procurement — all in one reception portal.
